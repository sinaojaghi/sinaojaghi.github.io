Hello! I'm Sina Ojaghi (سینا اوجاقی), a Ph.D. student in Computer Architecture at the University of Tehran, and a Research Assistant at the Institute for Research in Fundamental Sciences (IPM). My research bridges Hardware Security, Side-Channel Attacks on FPGAs, and Spiking Neural Networks for sustainable technologies.

With a track record as an instructor at Hamedan University of Technology and the University of Tehran, I've taught courses in AI, Computer Networks, and Research Methods. I also serve as Secretary and Speaker of the Cyber-Physical Systems Society of Iran (CPSSI).

My industry experience includes roles as Hardware Programmer at Afra Turbine Energy Co. and former IT Manager at HamVta Arya Co., where I applied my technical expertise to real-world embedded systems and infrastructure challenges.

IEEE DCHPC · 2026
IEEE Xplore
2026

This paper evaluates how placement of active wire fences affects the success of remote power side-channel attacks in multi-tenant FPGA environments, proposing countermeasure strategies for secure hardware sharing.

Object Detection for Vehicles with YOLO — Iranian Vehicle Dataset
IEEE SAMI · 2024 · 29,759 labeled images · 7 vehicle classes
IEEE Xplore
2024

A comprehensive vehicle detection benchmark with 29,759 labeled images across 7 classes. YOLOv7 achieves 85% precision and 85% mAP@0.5, emphasizing emergency vehicle movement facilitation for transportation research.

Iranian Vehicle Images Dataset for Object Detection Algorithm
Journal of AI and Data Mining · 2024 · 91.7% precision
Read Article
2024

A 3,000-image dataset of Iranian vehicles with 5,765 bounding boxes. YOLOv8s achieves 91.7% precision and 92.6% mAP@0.5 — a 10% improvement over COCO-trained models.
